Animal Decoded Publisher

A private publishing tool for the Animal Decoded YouTube channel

What is Animal Decoded?

Animal Decoded is an English YouTube Shorts channel about animals. Each Short covers one animal and its most surprising facts, every fact checked against at least two reliable sources, and ends with a clue to the next animal.

What does this tool do?

Animal Decoded Publisher is used only by the owner of the Animal Decoded channel, for that channel only. It:

  • uploads the channel's own finished videos to YouTube and schedules them,
  • sets their titles, descriptions, captions, thumbnails and playlists,
  • posts the channel's own comments under its own videos,
  • reads the channel's own view and audience retention statistics.

It is not offered to other creators and does not access anyone else's account.
